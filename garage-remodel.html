Our Approach

A GARAGE YOU WANT TO USE.

Most garages are the roughest room in the house: unfinished walls, a stained slab and years of patches nobody finished. It does not take a full contractor crew to fix that.

We repair what is damaged first, get the walls and ceiling smooth, paint everything, and finish with the epoxy floor so it goes down last and stays clean.

✦Damage fixed first, not painted over
✦Walls skim coated smooth before any paint goes on
✦Floor prepped properly so the epoxy bonds and lasts
✦One person start to finish, and the space left spotless
